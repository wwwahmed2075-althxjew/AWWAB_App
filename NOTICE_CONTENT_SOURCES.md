# AWWAB Islamic content references

This file records the primary references used for the starter offline daily-content and virtue cards. Hadith numbering can vary between editions and databases; the collection should be checked against the exact Arabic matn before release.

## Quran references
- Al-Baqarah 2:152 and 2:201
- Ar-Ra'd 13:28
- Al-Isra 17:9 and 17:82
- Al-Muzzammil 73:4 and 73:20
- Al-Ahzab 33:41
- Fatir 35:29
- Adh-Dhariyat 51:17

## Editorial safeguards
- Quran text must be checked against a verified Mushaf source; the app's Madinah Mushaf V4 page set remains separately integrity-checked by its SHA-1 manifest.
- Hadith text, attribution, and grading must be reviewed before release. Do not infer authenticity from a citation number alone.
- The 99-name list shown in the app is a common popular enumeration. Scholars have differed over identifying the complete enumeration; the app explicitly labels the list as a commonly circulated list and the meanings as concise explanations.
- The app's starter adhkar detail page is not yet a complete verified adhkar corpus. It must not be presented as exhaustive until all wording, repetition counts, and references are checked.
